# Comptabilité Maroc — Research Vault

An [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) vault of research notes on Moroccan accounting, tax and financial reporting: CGNC, CGI, Loi de Finances, AMMC publications and the primary documents behind them.

The notes are written in French, matching the terminology of the subject matter. The vault is meant to be opened in Obsidian, where the wikilinks, the canvas and the Excalidraw diagrams work.

## Start here

- **🏠 Accueil** — the home note and the map of content for the whole vault
- **Le Fil Conducteur** — the narrative thread that ties the topics together
- **🧵 Le Grand Récit — De la Facture à la Stratégie** — how an invoice becomes a strategy
- **📖 Lexique — Comprendre les rapports** — glossary of reporting terminology

The vault also ships a `Concept Graph.canvas`, an `Excalidraw` folder, `Obsidian-Templates` and a `Starter-Kit`.

## Using the vault

1. Clone the repository.
2. In Obsidian, choose *Open folder as vault* and select the cloned folder.
3. Start at the home note (**🏠 Accueil**).

The `.obsidian` folder is committed, so the plugin and appearance settings travel with the vault.

## Scope

Personal research notes for study and reference. Not professional, tax or legal advice, and not a substitute for the primary texts.
